Independent Post-Mortem Report (CBI-Ordered)

CBI/EX/023
Source: AIIMS Rishikesh, Dept. of Forensic Medicine • Body exhumed under CBI order • Second post-mortem conducted
All India Institute of Medical Sciences, Rishikesh
Department of Forensic Medicine & Toxicology
Post-Mortem Examination Report (Second Opinion)
Deceased:Vikram Singh, Male, 32 yrs
Referred by:CBI Special Unit, Dehradun
First PM conducted by:District Hospital, Mussoorie
Cause per first PM:"Injuries consistent with fall from height"
FINDINGS OF SECOND EXAMINATION:
1. Cranial injuries: Multiple fractures to occipital bone consistent with fall impact. However —
2. ADDITIONAL FINDING: A distinct depressed contusion on the left parietal region, measuring approximately 4cm x 2cm. Shape is linear with rounded edges — consistent with a cylindrical blunt instrument (e.g., a baton or lathi). This injury is NOT consistent with a fall. It shows characteristics of a direct, angled blow from behind and above.
3. Injury sequencing: Microscopic tissue analysis suggests the parietal contusion (finding #2) was sustained BEFORE the fall-related injuries. The victim was struck first, then fell.
4. Defensive injuries: Minor abrasions on right palm and forearm consistent with instinctive defensive posture. No skin or material under fingernails.
CONCLUSION: The deceased sustained a blow to the head from a blunt cylindrical instrument PRIOR to the fall. This injury would have caused disorientation and loss of balance. The fall was not accidental. Classification revised: HOMICIDE.
Sd/- Dr. P. Verma, MD (Forensic Medicine)
AIIMS Rishikesh
CBI Note: The first post-mortem — conducted under Inspector Devraj's supervision at Mussoorie District Hospital — completely missed a 4cm head wound inconsistent with a fall. Either gross incompetence or deliberate omission. A cylindrical blunt instrument. A lathi. Standard issue for every police officer in Uttarakhand. Inspector Devraj carries one.
