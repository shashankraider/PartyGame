Exhibit R

Land Registry — Shell Companies

CBI case file · document · Round 3

Land Registry — Shell Companies

Plots adjacent to Thakur Cottage bought up over 12 years by 'Highland Properties' — a chain of trusts and shells terminating in a trust controlled by Rajveer Bisht.

Land-registry extract showing the ownership history of plots adjacent to the Thakur estate from 2012 to 2024. Acquirer in each case: 'Highland Properties LLP', through three layers of holding trusts and proxy buyers. CBI investigators have traced the chain of ownership through each layer to a final beneficial owner: a private trust controlled by Rajveer Bisht. The Royal Pines Hotel was built on three of these plots.

Exhibit S

Bisht Family History — Hotel Licence Revocation, 2010

CBI/EX/019

CBI case file · document · Round 3

Bisht Family History — Hotel Licence Revocation, 2010

Records showing Harish Bisht's hotel licence was revoked at the personal request of Brigadier Thakur over a minor land-boundary dispute. Harish died of a heart attack six months later.

Archival records pulled from the district administration. In 2010, Harish Bisht — Rajveer's father — operated a small lodge near Landour Bazaar. A minor land-boundary dispute with the adjacent Thakur estate escalated when Brigadier Thakur used his army and district connections to have Harish's hotel licence revoked. Harish lost the lodge, his income, and his standing. He died six months later of a heart attack at age 52. Local opinion at the time was that 'he died of a broken heart'.

A voice memo recovered from Vikram's iCloud backup. Timestamped 11:42 PM, two nights before his death. He is alone, the audio is close-mic'd, occasional sounds of a pen tapping against a desk. The transcript reads: 'Okay. Okay. The Thakur thing keeps coming back. I can't make the episode work without it but I can't prove it. Bisht's land — that's clean on paper, all the registry stuff routes through shell companies but the timeline lines up too well, it's not a coincidence. And the rifle. The rifle is the rifle, I'm sure of it, I just can't say so on camera without — [pause] — Anya knows. Anya knows something. She won't sit down with me. Three times now. She's not avoiding me, she's avoiding the question. Hm. [pause] Rhea is going to be a problem when this drops. She'll see the numbers in the edit suite before anyone else does and she'll know I know. Channel sale, she thinks I haven't noticed, I've noticed. Whatever. [pause] Naina — I owe Naina a call. I owe her the call. She did the thing and I haven't even — god. [pause] Kabir, god, Kabir. The thesis thing is a sideshow but if I name him it'll come up and his career is — I haven't decided. I haven't decided. [pause] Devraj. Three pages. Three pages on a death investigation. He didn't even — [audio cuts off]' The recording ends abruptly. Battery log shows the phone hit 1 percent at that timestamp. The memo is filed in his Voice Memos app under no specific name — it appears next to dozens of other late-night thinking-aloud recordings he had made over the previous six months. This is just how he worked.
