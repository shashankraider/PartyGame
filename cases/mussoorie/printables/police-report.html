Mussoorie Police Station — Incident Report

CBI/EX/002
Uttarakhand Police
Mussoorie Police Station • District Dehradun
FIRST INFORMATION REPORT — UNNATURAL DEATH
FIR Number: MSR/UD/2026/0087
Date of Registration: [Date of discovery], 09:15 hrs
Investigating Officer: Inspector Devraj Khanna, Badge No. UK-4471
Deceased: Vikram Singh, Male, Age 32, R/o Cottage No. 14, Landour, Mussoorie
Location of Body: Base of ravine, Camel's Back Road, approx. 200m east of Gun Hill viewpoint
Time of Discovery: 06:05 hrs by morning jogger (Mr. Rakesh Negi, statement attached)
Estimated Time of Death: Between 20:00 and 22:00 hrs (previous evening)
SUMMARY OF FINDINGS:

Deceased appears to have fallen from Camel's Back Road through a damaged section of the iron safety railing at the sharp bend near Gun Hill. The railing at said location is old and corroded, with multiple maintenance complaints pending with the Municipal Board.

Weather conditions on the evening in question were poor — heavy mist, reduced visibility (est. 10-15 metres), wet path surface. Deceased was known to walk this route regularly after sunset for personal recreation.

No signs of foul play observed at the scene. No witnesses to the fall. Personal effects recovered: wallet (cash intact), camera bag with equipment, one shoe (left) found near railing.

Mobile phone not recovered.

CONCLUSION: Accidental death due to fall from height in adverse weather conditions. No further investigation warranted.
Inspector Devraj Khanna
Station House Officer, Mussoorie PS
CASE CLOSED
CBI Note: This report was filed within 6 hours of body discovery. No forensic team was called. No fingerprint analysis of railing. No canvassing of nearby shops for witnesses. The missing phone is noted but not investigated. Report is unusually brief for an unnatural death. — Why?
