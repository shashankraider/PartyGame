Exhibit M

Memo — Rhea Bhatia / Metropolis Media Inquiry

CBI/EX/013
Source: Vikram Singh's personal email (forwarded attachment) • Sender: Naina Kapoor • Subject: "Re: the thing you asked about — rough notes only" • Dated: 9 days before death • Two pages
FOR VS ONLY. NOT FOR PUBLICATION. SOURCING ON REQUEST.
1. ACQUISITION ACTIVITY
Metropolis Media (Delhi, est. 2019) is a creator-economy holding company. Two confirmed acquisitions of mid-sized Indian YouTube channels in the last 18 months. Their M&A lead is Dhruv Sehgal — spotted at three Delhi industry events in the last six weeks. He is being introduced to creators in a way that says he is shopping, not browsing.
2. RHEA-SPECIFIC
Two independent sources confirm Rhea has been in "preliminary conversations" with Sehgal for approximately four months. One source claims a term sheet exists. The second source will not be drawn on it. Separately: Rhea has been telling people at parties that the channel is "in transition." That phrasing is consistent with someone preparing to sell a brand they don't fully own.
3. WHAT I CAN'T TELL YOU
I cannot verify the financial picture without internal access. Pull the channel's Stripe statements yourself. Look at any unusual expense routing. Check whether Rhea's signature is on disbursements you did not authorise.

If she's doing what I think she's doing, the books will show it.
I can't see the books.

— N.
[handwritten in Vikram's hand, margin of section 2:] pull stripe
CBI Note: Forwarded to Vikram nine days before he died. He read it — highlights on two paragraphs, the marginal note "pull stripe" in his hand. He did not reply to the sender, did not forward it onward, and as far as can be determined did not pull the Stripe statements before his death. The memo confirms what Vikram suspected about Rhea and the channel sale. It cannot, on its own, prove embezzlement. The author is Naina Kapoor — the victim's ex-fiancée and, by profession, a corporate-investigations journalist.
