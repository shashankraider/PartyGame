I was closing my shop at approximately 8:15 PM on the evening in question. It was misty and I was pulling down the shutters. I saw a young woman standing near the entrance to Camel's Back Road, on the Kulri Bazaar side. She was looking down the road into the mist, as if waiting for someone or trying to see something.

I recognised her — she is a regular customer. Her name is Naina, she buys cosmetics and toiletries from my shop. She comes from a good family.

She stood there for maybe 5-7 minutes. At one point she seemed startled — she turned her head sharply as if she heard something from the direction of the road. A sound — I cannot say what. I was inside the shop and the shutter was making noise.

After that she walked away quickly toward the taxi stand. She did not go onto Camel's Back Road. She seemed... upset? Nervous? I am not sure of the right word.

I did not think anything of it at the time. Young people stand around. It is not unusual.
Sd/- Tilak Rawat
CBI Note: Places Naina Kapoor at the entrance to Camel's Back Road at 8:15 PM — directly contradicting her statement that she was at home. The shopkeeper says she "heard something" from the road. Estimated time of Vikram's fall: 8:20 PM. She was within earshot.
